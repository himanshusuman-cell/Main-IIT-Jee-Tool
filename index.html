Gulf & foreign nationals

Routes into an IIT from Dubai, Oman & the Gulf

Everything below is taken only from jeeadv.ac.in (Information Brochure 2026, foreign.html, admission_criteria.html) and iitdabudhabi.ac.ae. Pick the student's status and the tool lights up the routes that apply.

Which route applies to this student?

Route A JEE Advanced as a Foreign National / OCI-PIO (F) → any IIT + IISc

For: foreign passport holders; OCI/PIO cards issued on/after 04.03.2021; OCI/PIO cards issued before 04.03.2021 who CHOOSE the foreign option.
  • No JEE Main needed. Register directly for JEE Advanced (2026 window was 6 Apr – 2 May; foreign registration opens ~2 weeks before the Indian window).
  • Exam centre abroad: Abu Dhabi (F01) and Kathmandu (F02) are the only two foreign centres. Gulf students sit in Abu Dhabi.
  • Fee: USD 200 (SAARC) / USD 300 (non-SAARC) at a foreign centre; USD 100 / 200 if sitting in India.
  • Seats: supernumerary, capped at 10% of each course; outside all reservation (no EWS/OBC/SC/ST/PwD) and no female-supernumerary. Allotted only if the CRL rank ≤ the OPEN gender-neutral closing rank of that programme.
  • Eligibility B1–B4: born on/after 1 Oct 2001; max 2 attempts in 2 consecutive years; first Class XII appearance in 2025 or 2026 with PCM; never admitted to an IIT before. Armed-forces service relaxation with certificate.
  • Class XII for admission: ≥ 5 subjects with 75% aggregate, or board top-20 percentile. IB Diploma and GCE A-level are accepted equivalents; any other foreign board needs an AIU equivalence certificate. Grade-only boards must supply a marks-equivalence certificate.

Route B JEE Main + JEE Advanced as OCI/PIO (I) — treated at par with Indians

For: OCI/PIO cards issued BEFORE 04.03.2021 who choose the Indian option. Chosen once at registration — cannot be changed later.
  • Must write JEE Main and be in the top 2,50,000, then JEE Advanced — same as an Indian national.
  • Competes only in OPEN / OPEN-PwD (CRL); no EWS/OBC/SC/ST benefit; eligible for female supernumerary seats.
  • Fee: ₹1,600 (female / GEN-PwD) or ₹3,200 (GEN) in India; USD 200 at a foreign centre.
  • Applying both as OCI/PIO(I) via JEE Main and as OCI/PIO(F) directly = unfair means, candidature cancelled.
  • Note: choosing this option makes the student ineligible for CAET at IIT Delhi–Abu Dhabi.

Route C IIT Delhi – Abu Dhabi via CAET (no JEE at all)

For: UAE nationals; international students; OCI/PIO holders; Indian expatriates — Indian passport, living outside India for the last 5 years, Class VIII–XII completed outside India.
  • CAET 2026 sessions: 23 Nov 2025 · 8 Feb 2026 · 7 Jun 2026 · 12 Jul 2026 · Special drive 12 Aug 2026. Up to 2 sittings per year, best normalised score counts; may apply in max 2 academic years.
  • Centres: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Muscat, plus New Delhi, Singapore, Tokyo. Test 10:00–13:00 GST.
  • Eligibility: ≥ 75% in at least two of Physics/Chemistry/Maths AND ≥ 80% aggregate in Grade 12; first Grade 12 appearance 2025 or 2026; born on/after 1 Oct 2001; never admitted to an IIT.
  • Programmes (all CAA-accredited B.Tech, 4 yr): Computer Science & Engineering · Electrical Engineering (new 2026) · Chemical Engineering · Energy Engineering.
  • Scholarships: UAE nationals — 100% tuition + AED 4,000/month + housing + transport. Internationals & Indian expats — Tier I 100% tuition waiver at CGPA ≥ 8.0, Tier II 50% at CGPA ≥ 6.0 (top-5 per programme by CAET score at entry).
  • CAET paper (official brochure): 3 hrs, pen-and-paper OMR, English; 60 questions — 20 per subject (8 MCQ +4/−1, 6 single-digit integer +4/0, 3 paragraphs × 2 Qs +4/0); 80 marks per subject, 240 total. Qualify with ≥ 8/80 per subject AND ≥ 84/240 aggregate (or mean+SD if lower); score normalised to a 35–90 scale (PCAET per session, FCAET final).
  • Registration fee: AED 150 / US$ 41 per session (UAE nationals free). Merit lists are separate for UAE nationals and for Internationals + Indian expats; tie-break on Maths, then Physics marks.
  • Seats: the official brochure states the CAET seat matrix is published inside the seat-allocation portal (login required), not on the public pages — so no CAET seat number is quoted here. JoSAA seats for IITD-AD are shown in the panel below.
  • Apply only at admissions.abudhabi.iitd.ac.in · admissions@iitdabudhabi.ac.ae · +971 2 495 8510 · Khalifa City B, Abu Dhabi.

Route D Standard Indian route — JEE Main → JEE Advanced → JoSAA (all IITs, NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, incl. IIT Delhi–Abu Dhabi)

For: every Indian-passport holder (NRIs included) and OCI/PIO (I). Gulf students appear for JEE Main at NTA's overseas centres and JEE Advanced at Abu Dhabi.
  • JEE Main → top 2,50,000 → JEE Advanced → JoSAA (5 rounds). IITD-AD is a JoSAA choice under IIT Delhi: IIT Delhi tuition + AED 2,000/month stipend + housing-fee waiver.
  • Eligibility, attempts, 75%/top-20 rule are in the Academic planner and Exam pattern tabs. An NRI can run this AND DASA (Route E) in the same year.

Route E DASA — NITs, IIITs, IIEST, SPAs & GFTIs on the JEE Main rank alone (no JEE Advanced)

For: Foreign nationals, OCI/PIO holders, and NRIs (Indian passport with Class XI & XII done abroad within the last 8 years). Not for IITs. Portal: csab.nic.in. Coordinated by IIEST Shibpur.
  • Basis: JEE Main 2026 Paper 1 CRL (B.Arch → Paper 2A, B.Plan → Paper 2B). Seats are supernumerary, up to 15% of each programme, allotted by CRL among DASA applicants in 2 rounds — separate from JoSAA, so JoSAA closing ranks do not apply.
  • Eligibility: born on/after 1 Oct 2001; Class XII with Maths, Physics + one of Chemistry/Biotech/CS/Biology; 75% aggregate (or 7.5 CGPA) in 5 subjects; results by 30 Sept 2026 if awaiting. Board must be AIU-recognised (IB, A-level, Gulf 12-yr General Secondary, US HS Diploma listed; others need an AIU certificate).
  • Fee (DEF = registration + 1st-semester tuition): DASA Non-SAARC / NRI — US$ 300 + US$ 4,000 = US$ 4,300; SAARC nationals — US$ 2,300. The US$ 300 is non-refundable. Balance semesters paid to the institute.
  • 2026 timeline: registration 28 Jul – 3 Aug; Round-I result 6 Aug; Round-II 12 Aug; reporting 13–18 Aug. Willingness options: Freeze / Float / Slide / Surrender / Withdraw / Exit.
  • An NRI/OCI who already secured an NIT seat in JoSAA can still enter DASA — but a new DASA allotment cancels the JoSAA seat automatically. Securing an IIT seat in JoSAA bars JEE Advanced 2027.
  • Participating in our data: 31 NIT+IIEST (all except NIT Manipur), 18 IIITs, 22 GFTIs — plus DASA-only institutes not in JoSAA (DTU, NSUT, Manipal, Indian Maritime University, etc.).

Route F DASA-CIWG — Children of Indian Workers in the Gulf (one-third of DASA seats, near-domestic fees)

For: NRIs (Indian passport) whose either parent is working, or has worked, in a Gulf country till any date in 2026 — UAE, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia. OCI/PIO (I) are not eligible for CIWG.
  • Seats: 5% of each programme (i.e. one-third of the 15% DASA quota) reserved as CIWG seats; unfilled CIWG seats convert to Non-CIWG in Round II.
  • Fee: US$ 300 registration + ₹ 62,500 first-semester tuition (vs US$ 4,000 for general DASA). "Double tag" option: pay US$ 4,300 to be considered for both CIWG and Non-CIWG seats — balance refunded if admitted under CIWG.
  • Documents (Annexure 5): parent's passport, parent's visa valid on any date in 2026, parent's work permit, and an employer certificate (Annexure 12 format) — plus the standard DASA documents and proof of Class XI–XII abroad.
  • Everything else (eligibility, JEE Main basis, 75%, timeline) is the same as Route E. Counselling tip: list CIWG seats at the top of the choice list.

Foreign-national seat finder — which IITs, how many seats, what rank

How it actually works (jeeadv.ac.in): there is no separate list of "participating" IITs and no fixed foreign seat count. Every programme at all 23 IITs + IISc is open to foreign nationals and OCI/PIO (F). Their seats are supernumerary (created over and above the Indian intake), capped at 10% of each programme's seats, and a seat is allotted only if the candidate's CRL rank is equal to or better than that programme's OPEN gender-neutral closing rank. Gulf call guide — what to say, in order

1 · Open by finding the passport, not the marks

"Before we talk about colleges, one question decides everything: what passport does your child hold, and where did they do Class XI and XII? An Indian passport with schooling in the Gulf opens a route most parents here don't know about — I'll show you on screen."

2 · Name the three doors

"There are three separate doors, not one. JEE Advanced for the 23 IITs. JEE Main plus DASA for the NITs and IIITs — that's a quota only for students studying abroad. And CAET, IIT Delhi's own exam for its Abu Dhabi campus, with no JEE at all. Your child may be eligible for more than one."

3 · The CIWG line — usually the strongest card

"If either parent is working in the Gulf in 2026, your child qualifies for CIWG. One-third of all DASA seats are reserved for exactly this group, and the tuition is about ₹62,500 a semester instead of four thousand US dollars. Most families here never hear about it. We'll need the parent's visa, work permit and an employer letter."

4 · Correct the two most common mistakes

"Two things parents get wrong. First: DASA never applies to IITs — only NITs, IIITs and government institutes. Second: for an OCI card issued before March 2021 you must choose once, at registration, between Indian status and foreign status — and choosing Indian permanently closes CAET and CIWG. It cannot be undone."

5 · Explain the seat maths honestly

"Foreign seats at the IITs are a ceiling, not a promise — up to ten percent per branch, and only if the rank beats the open closing rank. DASA is up to fifteen percent. These are extra seats created on top; they don't take anything from Indian students. Let me show you the actual branch numbers."

6 · Fix the calendar in the parent's mind

"The order matters. JEE Main in January and April, JEE Advanced in May, JoSAA counselling June to July — five rounds. Only after that does DASA open, in late July, with two rounds in early August. If Class XII results are pending, they must reach the institute by 30 September. Miss a window and the year is gone."

7 · Close on the one exam that fits

"Based on what you've told me, here is the single route we should build around, and here is the score it needs. Everything else is a backup. Shall I put this on one page and send it to you tonight?"
