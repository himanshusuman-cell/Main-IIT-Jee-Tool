Gulf call guide — what to say, in order 1 · Open by finding the passport, not the marks "Before we talk about colleges, one question decides everything: what passport does your child hold, and where did they do Class XI and XII? An Indian passport with schooling in the Gulf opens a route most parents here don't know about — I'll show you on screen." 2 · Name the three doors "There are three separate doors, not one. JEE Advanced for the 23 IITs. JEE Main plus DASA for the NITs and IIITs — that's a quota only for students studying abroad. And CAET, IIT Delhi's own exam for its Abu Dhabi campus, with no JEE at all. Your child may be eligible for more than one." 3 · The CIWG line — usually the strongest card "If either parent is working in the Gulf in 2026, your child qualifies for CIWG. One-third of all DASA seats are reserved for exactly this group, and the tuition is about ₹62,500 a semester instead of four thousand US dollars. Most families here never hear about it. We'll need the parent's visa, work permit and an employer letter." 4 · Correct the two most common mistakes "Two things parents get wrong. First: DASA never applies to IITs — only NITs, IIITs and government institutes. Second: for an OCI card issued before March 2021 you must choose once, at registration, between Indian status and foreign status — and choosing Indian permanently closes CAET and CIWG. It cannot be undone." 5 · Explain the seat maths honestly "Foreign seats at the IITs are a ceiling, not a promise — up to ten percent per branch, and only if the rank beats the open closing rank. DASA is up to fifteen percent. These are extra seats created on top; they don't take anything from Indian students. Glossary — who is who: nationality & status terms TERM MEANING (OFFICIAL) Indian national Indian passport holder. Standard JEE Main → Advanced → JoSAA route; eligible for all reservations, female supernumerary. NRI Non-Resident Indian — Indian passport, living abroad. OCI/PIO (F) Card issued on/after 4 Mar 2021, or issued before and the student chooses foreign status: no JEE Main for IITs, direct JEE Advanced, 10% supernumerary IIT seats; DASA for NIT+. 4 March 2021 The cut-off date from the Supreme Court / Delhi High Court rulings that decides whether an OCI/PIO card holder may opt to be treated as Indian. Foreign national Not an Indian citizen (by birth or naturalisation). Direct JEE Advanced for IITs; DASA for NIT+; CAET for IITD-AD. UAE nationals are foreign nationals for India. SAARC Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka (and India). Nationals get lower JEE Advanced (US$100/200) and DASA (US$2,300) fees. Never reduces Indian seats. Opening / closing rank The best and worst rank admitted to a programme-category in a round. For foreigners at IITs, the OPEN gender-neutral closing rank is the bar. Quota and counselling rules RULE WHAT TO SAY Home State quota (HS) 50% of NIT seats are kept for candidates who passed the qualifying exam from that same state. IITs and IIITs have no home-state quota — every seat is All India. Other State quota (OS) The remaining 50% of NIT seats, open to candidates from every other state. Female supernumerary Roughly 20% extra seats created above the sanctioned strength. They do not reduce anyone else's seats. A female candidate is considered in both the gender-neutral and the female-only pool. Category reservation OBC-NCL 27% · SC 15% · ST 7.5% · EWS 10% · PwD 5% horizontal inside each category. Applies to both HS and OS quotas at the NITs. Choice locking options Freeze — keep the seat. Float — accept but stay in for a higher preference. Slide — same institute, better branch. Withdraw — exit counselling. Rounds — 5 only in 2026 Five JoSAA rounds; Round 5 (final) allotment 16 July 2026, acceptance deadline 20 July 2026. CSAB special rounds then fill seats still vacant at NITs, IIITs and GFTIs. There is no sixth JoSAA round. Preparatory course SC, ST and PwD candidates just below the IIT cutoff may be offered a one-year preparatory course. Those ranks carry a "P" suffix and sit on a separate rank list.
